## Bethesda category II ROM (F5 — mig_264 / mig_264b)

Operative-referral enrichment yields **18.9%** malignancy among Bethesda-2 patients (385 of 2,033 with `bethesda_final = 2`) before mig_264b hygiene. After **mig_264b** (24 non-malignant reclassifications; 19 FNA timing corrections), **residual ROM ~16.8%** (~342 patients). This remains above screening-cohort benchmarks because of tertiary surgical selection and cytologic uncertainty between follicular adenoma and carcinoma. Performance statistics (sensitivity, NPV, ROM by TR or Bethesda bucket) must be footnoted accordingly.
