## Two compounding defects in `manuscript_workspace.cohort_m044_ajcc_ete_v1`

### Defect 1 — Duplicate columns

`information_schema.columns` returns **64 rows** for what should be ~32 unique columns. Every column appears twice (some with different types, e.g. `surg_first_date` shows TIMESTAMP and DATE side-by-side). This is the fingerprint of a JOIN written as `SELECT a.*, b.*` without explicit projection. It will cause Cortex Analyst semantic-model binding to fail and risks silent COALESCE-style errors in any consumer.

### Defect 2 — `ete_grade_final` Boolean→VARCHAR cast artifacts

The post-rebuild distribution:

| ete_grade_final | n | Expected role |
|---|---:|---|
| `microscopic` | 2,413 | ✅ correct |
| `gross` | 1,239 | ✅ correct |
| `false` | 158 | ⚠️ should be `no_negative` |
| `present_ungraded` | 28 | ⚠️ ambiguous |
| `absent` | 15 | ⚠️ should be `no_negative` |
| (NULL) | 11 | OK |
| `true` | 4 | ⚠️ should be `gross` |

The M044 v5 manuscript used the strict-DTC analytic cohort with **n=68 no/negative ETE patients**. Post-rebuild, that group has been **completely eliminated** — split across `false`, `absent`, and possibly `present_ungraded` with `Boolean→string` cast artifacts. The total of `false` (158) + `absent` (15) is 173, which is larger than v5's 68; some of those "false" rows are likely true no-ETE-data records, but the lack of an explicit `no_negative` label means the M044 regression's reference structure has been silently corrupted.

This is the single largest threat to M044 v5's findings: the no/negative-vs-microscopic comparison (aOR 2.72; 95% CI 0.80–9.30) was already imprecise. If the no/negative cohort is silently misclassified, the Discussion's "no-negative ETE audit" paragraph is wrong.
