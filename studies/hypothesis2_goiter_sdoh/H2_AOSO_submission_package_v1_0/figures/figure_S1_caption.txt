Figure S1. Multiple imputation sensitivity analysis — complete-case vs MI-pooled race contrasts (H2 v3.2, Phase 3.4; m=20 imputations). Filled circles: MI-pooled Rubin's-rules estimates (IterativeImputer/BayesianRidge, burn-in=10 iterations, seed=42+i). Open circles: Phase 3.3 complete-case estimates (CC). Error bars: 95% CIs. All ORs vs White reference. Four panels: M1 (frank malignancy; CC n=518, MI n≈full cohort), M3 (PTC vs other malignant within malignant; CC n=159), M4 (indeterminate vs malignant; CC n=93), M2-Malignant (multinomial malignant vs benign contrast; CC n=518). Imputed covariates: BMI (79.5% missing), Bethesda category (53.5%), log(FNA days+1) (51.9%), log(gland weight) (13.7%). Smoking was not imputed (data never collected for this cohort; 99.9% unknown). Outcome variables were not imputed. Significance markers: *p<0.05; **p<0.01; ***p<0.001 (Wald z-test, pooled SE).
