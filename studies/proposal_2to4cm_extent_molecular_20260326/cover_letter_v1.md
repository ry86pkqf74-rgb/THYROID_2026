Dear Editors,

We submit for your consideration a **retrospective cohort study** examining **associations** between **preoperative characteristics** (including ultrasound-based nodule size between **2.0 and 4.0 cm**, **Bethesda category**, and **limited preoperative molecular testing**) and **initial total thyroidectomy** versus **initial lobectomy** among patients meeting prespecified eligibility in an integrated thyroid research database.

**What is new / contribution (associational only):**  
We report **adjusted odds ratios** from pre-specified parsimonious and extended logistic models on a **strict** preoperative nodal-exclusion cohort (**N = 558**) and show a **sensitivity** cohort with **broader** suspicious-node exclusion (**N = 635**). We document that a **pathology-size–defined** parallel cohort had **zero** eligible patients after the same strict nodal rule in this analytic freeze, and that **completion thyroidectomy** after lobectomy was **zero** under the pipeline’s completion flags in this cohort—all numbers traceable to CSV outputs in the study package.

**Design limitations we do not overstate:**  
The work is **observational**; we **do not** interpret findings as causal effects of molecular testing or cytology on surgical choice. Preoperative molecular testing was **infrequent (20/558)**.
