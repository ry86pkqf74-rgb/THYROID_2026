# Structured abstract — submission v1

**Title:** Initial thyroidectomy extent among adults with preoperative 2.0–4.0 cm thyroid nodules: a retrospective cohort study

**Background:** For 2–4 cm thyroid nodules, lobectomy and total thyroidectomy are both used. We describe practice patterns and **associations** between preoperative characteristics and **initial total thyroidectomy** versus **initial lobectomy** in a retrospectively assembled cohort.

**Methods:** Retrospective cohort from an integrated thyroid research database (analytic tables queried per study code in this folder; **no** database objects altered for manuscript preparation). **Primary cohort:** preoperative imaging-derived index nodule size **2.0–4.0 cm** on or before index surgery among first qualifying **hemithyroidectomy or total thyroidectomy** procedures, excluding **strict** preoperative nodal involvement (CT/MRI pathologic lymph nodes or Bethesda VI node FNA). **Sensitivity cohort:** same imaging window with **broad** suspicious-node exclusion (N=635). **Regression outcome:** indicator for initial total thyroidectomy. **Predictors (parsimonious):** age, sex, Bethesda ≥4, any preoperative molecular test; **extended** model added bilateral nodule indicator and TIRADS score. **Statistics:** Mann–Whitney U and chi-square/Fisher for univariable screens; multivariable logistic regression (statsmodels `Logit`) with complete cases per model; adjusted odds ratios (95% CI).

**Results:** **Primary analytic cohort N = 558** (**238** lobectomy, **320** initial total; **320/558 = 57.3%** initial total). **Broad cohort N = 635** (**375** initial total; **59.1%**). **Pathology-defined 2–4 cm cohort after strict nodal exclusion N = 0** in this frozen run. **Preoperative molecular testing:** **20/558 (3.6%)**. **Completion thyroidectomy after lobectomy:** **0/238** by pipeline completion flags. In adjusted primary-cohort models, **Bethesda ≥4** was associated with higher odds of initial total (parsimonious **aOR 2.74**, **95% CI 1.81–4.15**, **p ≈ 1.7 × 10⁻⁶**); **age** was associated with lower odds (per-year **aOR 0.986**, **CI 0.975–0.998**, **p = 0.026**). **Sex** and **preoperative molecular testing** were **not** significantly associated in adjusted primary models. **Extended model:** **bilateral nodule indicator aOR 2.01** (**CI 1.28–3.13**, **p = 0.002**). Broad-cohort parsimonious model was **directionally similar** (e.g. **Bethesda ≥4 aOR 2.77**, **CI 1.88–4.07**).

**Conclusions:** In this imaging-defined 2–4 cm preoperative cohort with strict nodal exclusion, **initial total thyroidectomy** was common; **cytologic risk category** showed the strongest adjusted association with extent **among measured variables**. Findings are **associational**; confounding and single-database limits apply. Molecular testing was **infrequent**; subgroup modeling is **not** definitive.
