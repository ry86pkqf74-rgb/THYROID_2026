# Structured abstract — submission v1

**Title:** Initial thyroidectomy extent among adults with preoperative 2.0–4.0 cm thyroid nodules: a retrospective cohort study

**Background:** For ultrasound-defined **2–4 cm** thyroid nodules, lobectomy and total thyroidectomy both remain common. We describe **associations** between preoperative characteristics and **initial total thyroidectomy** versus **initial lobectomy**—**not** long-term oncology outcomes.

**Methods:** Retrospective cohort from an integrated thyroid research database (analytic tables queried per study code in this folder; **no** database objects altered for manuscript preparation). **Primary cohort:** preoperative imaging-derived index nodule size **2.0–4.0 cm** on or before index surgery among first qualifying **hemithyroidectomy or total thyroidectomy** procedures, excluding **strict** preoperative nodal involvement (CT/MRI pathologic lymph nodes or Bethesda VI node FNA). **Sensitivity cohort:** same imaging window with **broad** suspicious-node exclusion (N=635). **Regression outcome:** indicator for initial total thyroidectomy. **Predictors (parsimonious):** age, sex, Bethesda ≥4, any preoperative molecular test; **extended** model added bilateral nodule indicator and TIRADS score. **Statistics:** Mann–Whitney U and chi-square/Fisher for univariable screens; multivariable logistic regression (statsmodels `Logit`) with complete cases per model; adjusted odds ratios (95% CI). **Reporting:** STROBE-oriented; main figures: CONSORT-style cohort flow and parsimonious-model forest plot (`fig1_cohort_flow_publication.png`/`.pdf`, `fig2_forest_primary_publication.png`/`.pdf`; legends **`figure_legends_v2.md`**). Legacy 150 DPI pipeline bars (`fig_cohort_flow.png`, `fig_forest_total_vs_lobectomy.png`) are for internal replication only.

**Results:** **Primary analytic cohort N = 558** (**238** lobectomy, **320** initial total; **320/558 = 57.3%** initial total). **Broad cohort N = 635** (**375** initial total; **59.1%**). **Pathology-defined 2–4 cm cohort after strict nodal exclusion N = 0** in this frozen run. **Preoperative molecular testing:** **20/558 (3.6%)**. **Completion thyroidectomy (secondary ascertainment-quality finding):** Among **238** initial lobectomy patients, **0** had later completion captured by the **OED-only** operative-episode pipeline, **25/238 (10.5%)** had **path-synoptic definite** later completion, **26** had **any** later thyroid-related event, and **1** remained **ambiguous** (**Table 4**; `table7_completion_thyroidectomy.csv`; `completion_audit_outputs/`). In adjusted primary-cohort models, **Bethesda ≥4** was associated with higher odds of initial total (parsimonious **aOR 2.743**, **95% CI 1.814–4.147**, **p ≈ 1.7 × 10⁻⁶**); **age** was associated with lower odds (per-year **aOR 0.986**, **CI 0.975–0.998**, **p = 0.026**). **Female sex** (**aOR 0.974**, **p = 0.91**) was **not** significantly associated in adjusted primary parsimonious models. For **preoperative molecular testing** (**aOR 0.606**, **p = 0.29**), **no statistically significant association was observed; however, interpretation is limited by sparse testing (20/558) and potential selection bias.** **Extended model:** **bilateral nodule indicator aOR 2.005** (**CI 1.282–3.134**, **p = 0.0023**). Broad-cohort parsimonious model was **directionally similar** (e.g. **Bethesda ≥4 aOR 2.765**, **CI 1.878–4.071**).

**Conclusions:** In this imaging-defined 2–4 cm preoperative cohort with strict nodal exclusion, **initial total thyroidectomy** was common; **cytologic risk category** showed the strongest adjusted association with extent **among measured variables**. Findings are **associational**; confounding and single-database limits apply. Molecular testing was **infrequent**; concordance summaries are **exploratory** only.
