  - vascular_invasion
      ONLY use when the pathologist explicitly describes tumor cells within a BLOOD VESSEL
      (vein, venule, artery, arteriole, angioinvasion). Must NOT be used for lymphatic channels.
      Synonyms that ARE pure vascular: "angioinvasion", "blood vessel invasion", "intravascular invasion"

  - lymphatic_invasion
      ONLY use when the pathologist explicitly describes tumor cells within a LYMPHATIC CHANNEL
      and the report clearly distinguishes it from vascular. Lymphatic channels have no red blood
      cells and no smooth muscle wall.
      entity_value: present | absent

  - lymphovascular_invasion
      USE THIS when the pathologist uses a COMBINED or UNDIFFERENTIATED term that does NOT
      distinguish vascular from lymphatic. This is the correct type for ALL of the following terms:

        "angiolymphatic invasion", "lymph-vascular invasion", "lymphatic/vascular invasion",
        "lymph vascular invasion", "vascular or lymphatic invasion",
        "angiolymphatic space invasion"
      DO NOT split lymphovascular_invasion into separate vascular_invasion + lymphatic_invasion.
      entity_value: present | absent

      Numeric count of vessels/foci involved (if specified). e.g. "4 vessels", "more than 4"
      entity_value: the number or description as stated

  - capsular_invasion
      Tumor penetration of the thyroid capsule.
      entity_value: present | absent | minimal | extensive | cannot_assess

  - perineural_invasion_detailed
      Perineural invasion present/absent; include named nerve or count if specified.
      entity_value: present | absent

  - soft_tissue_invasion
      Extrathyroidal extension (ETE) into strap muscles or other soft tissues.
      entity_value: present | absent

  - ki67_index
      Ki-67 proliferation index (numeric %, from IHC).
      entity_value: the percentage as stated

  - mitotic_rate
      Mitotic figures per 10 HPF.
      entity_value: as stated

  - necrosis
      Tumor necrosis.
      entity_value: present | absent | focal | extensive

  - ptnm_stage
      Pathologic TNM stage if explicitly stated.
      entity_value: the stage string (e.g. "pT3bN1aM0")
