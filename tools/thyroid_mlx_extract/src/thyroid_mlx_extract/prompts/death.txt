- cancer_specific: death attributable to thyroid cancer or its complications (e.g., airway obstruction from local recurrence, distant metastatic disease, complications of cancer therapy that wouldn't have occurred otherwise).

- uncertain: insufficient information to attribute either way.

Be conservative: require explicit evidence to attribute cancer-specific. Prefer "uncertain" over a stretched attribution.

Include:
- proximate_cause as a brief phrase
- contributing factors as a list of phrases
- death_date if present (ISO format)
- location_of_death if stated
- last_treatment_active: was the patient on active cancer therapy at the time of death?
- evidence_text: verbatim source substring(s) supporting your attribution
- confidence: 0.5 for uncertain edge cases, 0.9+ for unambiguous

OUTPUT: single JSON matching schema.
